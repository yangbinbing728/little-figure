A FIELD GUIDE TO EVERYDAY MOVEMENT

Little Figures,
Big City

A small map of the many ways we move through New York.

Little Figures, Big City is an interactive website about everyday movement in New York City. It looks at how walking, riding, and taking the subway shape the way we experience the city.

Choose a way to move and follow a route through familiar streets and neighborhoods. Along the way, photographs, places, and small details invite you to pause and notice the city from a different point of view.

The project is a personal map of urban life: part journey, part visual archive, and part invitation to explore at your own pace.
